Host tip: the app runs in a container, so localhost/127.0.0.1 refer to the container, not your laptop. Use host.docker.internal for DBs on your machine or via an SSH tunnel. (We'll auto-translate loopback hosts for you.)

Adding a database introspects its schema into a DSL file. Usually completes in a few seconds; larger schemas (hundreds of tables) may take 10-30 seconds.
